The Endeavour difference

Every child deserves
to be understood.

At Endeavour International School, we believe education should do more than prepare students for examinations. It should help them discover who they are and what they can contribute.

Founded by educator Dr. Harshvardhan Shrivastva, our school brings a thoughtful, student-centred approach to learning in Bhopal. We aim to create an environment where strong foundations, independent thinking and kindness grow together.

Academic affiliation

Affiliated with CISCE

Endeavour International School is affiliated with the Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations (CISCE). CISCE conducts the ICSE examination at Class 10 and the ISC examination at Class 12.
